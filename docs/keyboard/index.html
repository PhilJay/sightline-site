#Everywhere

DefaultAction
⇧⌘3Capture full screen
⇧⌘4Capture area
⇧⌘5All-in-one: screen, window, area or scrolling, with a timer
⇧⌘1Capture window
⇧⌘2Repeat last area
⇧⌘6Scrolling capture
⇧⌘CCapture text
⇧⌘7Record area
⇧⌘8Save replay (macOS 27, while Replay is on)
⌃⌥HHistory
⌃⌥DShow differences
⌃⌥TStop tracing
noneCapture area with timer, Pick color, Edit image from clipboard: set your own

Until you turn off the macOS screenshot shortcuts, ⇧⌘3, ⇧⌘4 and ⇧⌘5 open both tools. Settings > Shortcuts shows how, and Restore Defaults puts every shortcut back.

On macOS 27 with Siri AI turned on, ⇧⌘6 is also the macOS shortcut for Ask Siri about selected area, and macOS gets it first, so scrolling capture doesn't start. Turn it off in System Settings > Keyboard > Keyboard Shortcuts > Screenshots, or give scrolling capture another shortcut.

#History

KeyAction
⌃⌥HOpen or close History
← → ↑ ↓Move the selection. Left and right move the text cursor instead while you type a search.
ReturnCopy the selected capture
Space or ⌘YQuick Look, and close it again. While you type a search, use ⌘Y.
⌘DAdd to Favorites or remove from them
⌘⌫Move to Trash. After typing a search, click the card first. Then plain ⌫ works too.
⌘+ ⌘-Larger or smaller previews, 2 to 4 per row. A pinch on the trackpad does the same.
EscClose Quick Look, then History
