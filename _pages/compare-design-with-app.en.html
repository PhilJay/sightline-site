A design overlay shows the mockup on top of the build, but you still have to find what is off. Sightline compares the design with your app for you: pin the design over the running app, turn on Show Differences, and every mismatch gets a number.
  • Numbered differences, each marked with a box
  • A reason for each: shifted, only in the pin, only in the app or looks different
  • Lined up first, so a small offset of the whole pin doesn’t count
  • Marked or Difference view to see the mismatch itself
  • Copy result: the marked image and the list in one go
    •
  • Pin the designSelect the frame in Figma or any mockup with ⇧⌘4 and press ⌘P.
  • Show DifferencesDrag the pin over the running app and choose Show Differences in its bar.
  • Fix with Tracing onClicks go through to the app below and the marks follow your changes.
    •

    An earlier screenshot works too: pin it from History to see what changed.

    Sightline compares what is below the pin, so it doesn’t matter what draws it: a web page in any browser, the iOS Simulator or a Mac app.

    Claude Code, Codex or Cursor can run the same check through Sightline’s MCP server, fix the code and check again until no differences are left. See how Claude Code checks your app against the design and the compare_with_design reference.
