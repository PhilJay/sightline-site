Screenshots and recording for macOS

Capture, record, annotate, done. For you and your AI agent.

A menu bar screenshot and screen recording tool with a slim editor, scrolling capture and Remove Background, plus a built in MCP server so your AI agent can see what it built.

Optimized for macOS 27

Capture and annotate

Capture. Mark it up.

Area, window and full screen with the shortcuts you already know, a magnifier for pixel exact edges and a floating thumbnail. While you drag, ⌘C, ⌘S, ⌘P and ⌘B copy, save, pin or cut out the background right away.

The slim editor draws arrows, shapes, text, highlights, spotlight, magnify, blur and step numbers, each on a single key. It also opens the PNG, JPEG or MP4 files you already have.

Built for AI agents

Your agent sees what it built.

Sightline bundles an MCP server, so your agent can capture the screen, list windows, read text, annotate and open a shot in the editor. It builds a button, takes a look, notices it sits 8 px off and fixes it.

Claude Code, Claude Desktop and Codex CLI connect with one click in Settings. Every mark an agent draws stays editable for you.

Remove Background

Lift the subject.

Remove Background cuts a capture's subject out onto a transparent background. Press ⌘B while you drag, or use the tool in the editor: click what you want to keep, click again to add more, Option click to take away.

Apple's on-device Vision model. Needs macOS 27.

Scrolling capture

Capture the whole page.

Long pages, chat logs and Terminal output become one tall image. Sightline scrolls for you, or you scroll yourself, up or down, and it follows along and stitches the frames together.

Screen recording

Blur that follows.

Record an area, a window or the whole display to MP4 with system audio. The clip opens in the same editor: arrows, text and blurs for the whole clip or a few seconds of it, then trim the ends.

Cover an email address once and the blur stays on it while the page scrolls. No keyframes, nothing to line up.

Recording needs macOS 15.

And everything around it.

Spots credentials before you share

A password, an API key or a token is found and its value blurred while its name stays readable. The key in the tool bar highlights what it found. Cover everything at once, or Choose them one by one.

Apple Intelligence on device. macOS 26 or later.

Names you can search

Instead of a row of timestamps, a capture is named after what is in it, like stripe-webhook-401.png.

Apple Intelligence on device. macOS 26 or later.

Pin it to the screen

Any capture floats over your other windows at full size. Drag it, pinch to resize, and change its opacity. Tracing lets clicks reach the app below, and Focus hides every other pin.

Never lose a capture

⌃⌥H opens a searchable history of everything you copied or saved, with copy, edit, pin and delete. The last 24 hours are free.

Capture Text and QR codes

Select an area and the text in it lands on your clipboard. QR codes and barcodes are read too, and nothing leaves your Mac.

Paste and arrange

Paste images and text into the editor with ⌘V or drop an image file in. Move, resize and layer them with Bring to Front and Send to Back.

Replay

Keep the last 15 seconds of your screen and save what just happened with ⌘⇧8.

Needs macOS 27.

Shortcuts and Spotlight

Captures can start a Shortcuts workflow, and saved screenshots show up in Spotlight by name.

Private by design.

On-device

Screenshots stay on your Mac. Text recognition, naming and the credential check run on device. No analytics and no server of its own.

No account

Nothing to sign up for and nothing to sign in to. Purchases go through the App Store.

You approve every agent

Agent access is off until you turn it on, and each new agent asks first. Every capture plays the sound and names the agent. Agents can't change settings and only work inside your screenshot folder.

Works like the macOS screenshot tool. Then keeps going.

Same shortcuts, same thumbnail in the corner, nothing to relearn. Sightline takes over the built in shortcuts and adds the parts that were missing.
